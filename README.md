## What is Orbiton?

There is a quiet revolution happening, and most builders can feel it before they can explain it.

For years, deploying on blockchain had a developer's shape. A team of engineers. A complex stack of tools. Someone who could, in theory, navigate it all. That world is changing. The barrier between an idea and a live, functioning protocol is no longer just technical — it is structural. It is slow. It is fragmented.

This is the reason **Orbiton** exists. Not as a reaction to a single failed launch or a single broken bridge — but as a response to where everything is clearly heading.

## The Problem We Solve

The blockchain ecosystem is fragmented.

- Ethereum. BSC. Solana. Arbitrum. Base.
- Dozens of chains, each with its own tools and standards.
- Builders manage multi-chain liquidity and disconnected tools — all at once.

**That is not a developer problem. That is an infrastructure problem.**

Orbiton was designed to fix it.
